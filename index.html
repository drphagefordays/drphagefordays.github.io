About

Phages as tools, therapeutics and probes of biology

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Fossati Laboratory, Department of Microbiology, Tumor and Cell Biology, at Karolinska Institutet and SciLifeLab. My experience covers de novo phage genome engineering and high-throughput host–pathogen interaction studies.

I completed my Ph.D. in Food Science and Microbiology at Cornell University, engineering recoded, bio-orthogonal bacteriophages for pathogen capture and detection. A postdoctoral position at the Carnegie Institution for Science followed, where I studied uncultivated double-stranded RNA phages from Yellowstone hot springs, before I joined KI and SciLifeLab in 2025.

Research

Current themes

Where viral genomics, structure prediction and mass spectrometry meet.

Phage discovery against M. abscessus

Isolating, sequencing and characterising novel virulent mycobacteriophages from municipal wastewater, including phage Freja, and testing them against multidrug-resistant clinical isolates in smooth and rough morphotypes.

Host rangeKill curvesGenome sequencing

Phage control of host RNA polymerase

Integrating AlphaFold3 predictions with SEC-DIA-MS and targeted AP-MS to identify phage regulators such as gp52 and gp53 that act on host transcription complexes.

Phage genomics and transcription maps

Promoter architectures and transcriptional pause sites in phage genomes, mapped with viral genomics and cappable-seq.

Engineered and recoded phages

Whole-genome recoded bacteriophages with non-canonical amino acids for bio-orthogonal capture and luciferase-reporter biosensing of low-abundance pathogens in water.

The principle

From DNA to a new phage

How a phage turns a bacterium into a factory for more phages, and the tools we use to study each step.

  1. DNA

    The phage genome

    Injected into the bacterium, it carries the instructions for every new phage.

    Golden GateGibson / HiFiBREDCRISPR-CasTnSeqGenome sequencing
  2. Transcription

    RNA polymerase copies genes into RNA.

  3. RNA

    Messenger RNA

    A working copy of each gene, ready to be read.

    RNA-seqCappable-seqPromoter mapping
  4. Translation

    The host's ribosomes read the RNA.

  5. Protein

    Phage proteins

    Capsid, tail and lysis enzymes, built by the host cell.

    AP-MSSEC-DIA-MSAlphaFold3Functional characterization
  6. Assembly

    Proteins package new copies of the genome.

  7. Phage

    New phages

    The cell bursts and releases the next generation.

    Plaque assaysHost-range screeningKill curvesMIC

Adsorption

Experience

Research appointments

  1. SciLifeLab Clinical Translational Postdoctoral Researcher2025 – Present

    Fossati Laboratory, Department of Microbiology, Tumor and Cell Biology · Karolinska Institutet and SciLifeLab, Stockholm

    • Isolated, sequenced and characterised novel virulent mycobacteriophages from municipal wastewater that are active against multidrug-resistant clinical M. abscessus.
    • Combined AlphaFold3 structure predictions with SEC-DIA-MS and targeted AP-MS to identify phage-encoded regulators (gp52/gp53) of host RNA polymerase transcription complexes.
    • Co-supervised Master's students and mentored junior technicians in cloning, phage propagation and proteomic data processing.
  2. Postdoctoral Researcher2024 – 2025

    Carnegie Institution for Science · Stanford, CA

    • Led molecular genetics and functional genomics on uncultivated dsRNA bacteriophages infecting thermophilic, photosynthetic bacterial communities in Yellowstone hot springs.
    • Built high-throughput sequencing and targeted RT-PCR workflows to reconstruct fragmented environmental viral genomes from low-input metagenomic samples.
  3. Graduate Research Assistant2019 – 2024

    Nugen Research Group, Department of Food Science · Cornell University, Ithaca, NY

    • Engineered whole-genome recoded bacteriophages with non-canonical amino acids for site-specific chemical functionalisation and bioconjugation.
    • Developed phage-based magnetic separation and luciferase-reporter biosensors able to detect low-abundance pathogens in agricultural and municipal water.
    • Ran high-throughput phage mutagenesis, plaque-morphology tracking and burst-kinetics studies across Gram-negative and Gram-positive hosts.
