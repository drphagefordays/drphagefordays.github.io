About

Phages as tools, therapeutics and probes of biology

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Fossati Laboratory, Department of Microbiology, Tumor and Cell Biology, at Karolinska Institutet and SciLifeLab. My experience covers de novo phage genome engineering and high-throughput host–pathogen interaction studies.

Research

Current themes

Where phage biology, genomics and proteomics meet.

Phage discovery against M. abscessus

Isolating, sequencing and characterising novel virulent mycobacteriophages from municipal wastewater, including phage Freja, and testing them against multidrug-resistant clinical isolates in smooth and rough morphotypes.

Host rangeKill curvesGenome sequencing

Phage control of host RNA polymerase

Using SEC-DIA-MS and targeted AP-MS, with AlphaFold3 predictions as support, to identify phage regulators such as gp52 and gp53 that act on host transcription complexes.

Phage genomics and transcription maps

Promoter architectures and transcriptional pause sites in phage genomes, mapped with viral genomics and cappable-seq.

Engineered and recoded phages

Whole-genome recoded bacteriophages with non-canonical amino acids for bio-orthogonal capture and luciferase-reporter biosensing of low-abundance pathogens in water.

Phage engineering

How a phage genome is built by Golden Gate assembly

A whole genome is assembled from plasmid-based fragments in a single reaction, then transformed into host cells to produce phage.

1 · Build the parts

A phage genome is split into fragments, and each fragment is cloned on its own plasmid. Every part carries Type IIS restriction sites that define unique four-base overhangs at its ends.

Proteomics

How AP-MS finds the partners of unknown phage proteins

Many phage proteins have no known function. Pulling a tagged protein out of the cell together with its interaction partners shows what it works with.

1 · Unknown proteins

Many phage genes encode proteins with no known function. Their sequences give few clues, so one way to learn what they do is to find out what they interact with.

Proteomics

How SEC-MS maps protein interactions

Size-exclusion chromatography keeps protein complexes intact and sorts them by size. Mass spectrometry then reads out which proteins are in each fraction, and the elution profiles reveal who interacts with whom.

Infect

A culture of bacteria is infected with phage. Each phage attaches to a cell and injects its genome, and the cells are harvested at a chosen time after infection.
